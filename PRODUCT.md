# Proctal

Personal Windows interview-practice app. Listens to selected call output, optionally captures the candidate's microphone as separate live captions, and generates text assistance from Azure gpt-6-luna. Context includes résumé, job description, notes and explicit screenshot submissions. No voice answers. F10 captures, F11 submits, F12 toggles the overlay.

User: a candidate rehearsing interviews who needs readable answers and predictable controls while their attention is on another window. Show failures honestly and keep setup separate from the compact overlay.

Native Windows 11 x64, WPF, .NET 10. No webview dependency. Preserve resizing, keyboard navigation, accessible names, file pickers and high-DPI support. Capture exclusion is conditional, not complete invisibility. Dependencies and data stay local and support clean removal.

User's visual commitment: modern, friendly UI comparable in craft to Cluely and Parakeet AI, with deliberate fonts and colors. No ornamental marketing sections or generic visual filler. Original branding and implementation.
